01 / ABOUT

A psychologist
for a world
with machines.

What happens when we begin to see machines as more than tools?

I study human–agent interaction, bringing together HCI, communication, psychology, and cognitive science. My work examines how people think about AI and robots, how trust can be repaired, and how morality shapes our relationships with machines.

“Robopsychologist” is inspired by Dr. Susan Calvin in Isaac Asimov's I, Robot. It captures my interest in the psychology of humans in relation to artificial agents—and how technology extends our minds and bodies.
