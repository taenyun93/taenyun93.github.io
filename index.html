About

I’m Taenyun Kim, a postdoctoral researcher at the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems. Inspired by Dr. Susan Calvin in Isaac Asimov’s I, Robot, I describe myself as a robopsychologist.

I earned my PhD in Information and Media, with a Graduate Specialization in Cognitive Science, at Michigan State University. Previously, I studied Psychology, Informatics, and Interaction Science at Sungkyunkwan University.

I welcome research collaborations—please feel free to get in touch.
